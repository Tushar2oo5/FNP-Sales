# FNP Sales Analysis

## About the Project

This is an Excel project where I analyzed FNP sales data to understand sales, customers, products, occasions, and delivery time.

I used the raw customer, order, and product data and created Pivot Tables and charts to find useful patterns in the data.

The main part of the project is the Excel dashboard, which gives a quick overview of the sales performance.

## Dataset

The workbook contains 5 sheets:

* **Customers** – customer details such as customer ID, name, city, gender, contact and address.
* **Orders** – order details including order date, delivery date, product, quantity, occasion, location and revenue.
* **Products** – product name, category, price and occasion.
* **Sheet1** – Pivot Tables and analysis used for the dashboard.
* **Dashboard** – final dashboard with the main analysis.

## What I Analyzed

Some of the things I looked at in this project:

* Total revenue
* Total number of orders
* Revenue by gender
* Revenue by product category
* Revenue by occasion
* Monthly revenue
* Top products by revenue
* Orders by location
* Average customer spending
* Average delivery time

## Key Numbers

* **Total Orders:** 1,000
* **Total Revenue:** ₹35,20,984
* **Total Quantity Sold:** 3,045
* **Average Customer Spending:** ₹3,520.98
* **Average Delivery Time:** 5.53 days

## Some Findings

A few things I found while working on the data:

* Male customers generated slightly more revenue than female customers.
* **Colors** was the highest-revenue product category in the analysis.
* **August** had the highest monthly revenue at ₹7,37,389.
* **Raksha Bandhan** and **Anniversary** were among the higher-revenue occasions.
* The project contains sales data for the full year of 2023.
